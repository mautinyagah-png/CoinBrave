About CoinBrave

CoinBrave is a demo cryptocurrency trading platform built for learning and experimenting with a real trading UI — without touching real money.

How it works

  • 📊 Live prices from the public CoinGecko API — same data real platforms use
  • 💰 $5,000 demo balance on signup, plus a starter portfolio of 6 coins
  • 📈 Real trading flow — buy/sell any coin with a 0.1% simulated fee
  • 🎯 Price alerts that actually trigger when markets move
  • 📜 Full history of every trade, deposit, and withdrawal
  • 💾 Everything stored in your own browser — no server, no tracking

FAQ

Is this real money?

No. All balances are simulated. No real crypto is bought, sold, or held. This is purely a demo.

Where is my data stored?

Locally in your browser using localStorage. It never leaves your device. Clearing browser data erases your account.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Not yet — accounts are per-browser. Multi-device sync requires a real backend (planned).

How are prices updated?

Prices refresh every 60 seconds from CoinGecko. Charts use cached data for the last hour to avoid rate limits.

What's P/L?

Profit/Loss. CoinBrave tracks your average buy price per coin. When the current price is higher, you see green (profit). Lower, red (loss).

Can I delete my account?

Yes — Settings → Danger Zone → Delete My Account. This erases everything from your browser forever.
