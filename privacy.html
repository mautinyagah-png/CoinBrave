Privacy Policy

Last updated:

CoinBrave is a demonstration project. It is not a real exchange and does not handle real money or real cryptocurrency.

What we store: your name, email, hashed password, wallet balances, trade history, alerts, notifications, and profile avatar — all stored locally in your browser's localStorage. None of this data is sent to any server we control.

What we don't do: we don't sell your data, we don't track you across sites, we don't share anything with advertisers.

Third parties: live prices come from the public CoinGecko API. Chart fallback uses allorigins.win as a public CORS proxy. These services may log request metadata (IP, user agent) as part of their normal operation.

Your control: you can clear all your data at any time by clearing your browser's site data. There is no server-side deletion because there is no server.

Contact: questions? Open an issue on the project repository.
